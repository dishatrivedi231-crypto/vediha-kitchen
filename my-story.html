Meet the Baker

My Story

How a curious little girl became a baker who bakes for the people she loves.

When I was small, my sister and my mom used to bake together — and I always wanted to be right there with them.

Curiosity
I used to bake with them, watching flour and butter turn into something you could actually eat. Then one day, my mom was busy and told me I should start baking alone. So I baked alone — and I really enjoyed it. I learned a lot from my mom and sister, especially when we filmed our YouTube videos making mug cakes and banana cakes. Now it's just my favourite hobby.

Struggle
It didn't always go to plan. Sometimes the cakes don't turn out properly and it makes a huge mess. But I realised something that I still believe today: you won't learn if you don't make mistakes. Every messy bake just showed me what to try differently next time.

Care
Somewhere along the way, baking stopped being just for me. I focus on sugarless baking because my mom doesn't eat sugar, so I engineer special cakes just for her. And a lot of my friends don't eat eggs, so I make sure my cakes are eggless, so they can enjoy them too. I just want to make people happy.

Purpose
I really liked baking day-to-day, but we always just ate it ourselves or gave it to friends at birthday parties. Since they were really tasty, I thought — why not donate to some charities? I figured they might want some delicious treats too. I don't know for sure if I want to become a professional chef, but I do know that baking will always, always be my hobby.

In Her Own Words

A Chat with Vedhica

How did you first get into baking?

Vedhica: When I was small, my sister and my mom used to bake together, and I used to bake with them. But then one day, my mom was busy and told me I should start baking alone. So, I baked alone, and I really enjoyed it! I learned a lot from my mom and sister, especially when we filmed our YouTube videos making mug cakes and banana cakes. Now, it's just my favorite hobby.

Why did you decide to start baking for charity?

Vedhica: I really liked baking day-to-day, but we always just ate it ourselves or gave it to friends at birthday parties. Since they were really tasty, I thought, why not donate to some charities? I figured they might want some delicious treats, too!

You specialize in eggless and sugar-free treats. Why is that?

Vedhica: I focus on sugarless baking because my mom doesn't eat sugar, so I engineer special cakes just for her. And a lot of my friends don't eat eggs, so I make sure my cakes are eggless so they can enjoy them too. I just want to make people happy!

Who were your very first "customers"?

Vedhica: My American Girl dolls, Lucy and Sophie! It was their birthday on the 5th of June. My mom and I baked a special cake for them, put some flowers next to it, and sang Happy Birthday.

Do you ever have baking disasters?

Vedhica: Yeah, a few times! Sometimes the cakes don't turn out properly and it makes a huge mess. But I realized that you won't learn if you don't make mistakes.

What is a normal weekend morning like for you?

Vedhica: I love eating pancakes on weekends, so I always get up and make them completely independently first thing in the morning.

Do you want to be a professional chef when you grow up?

Vedhica: I'm in Year 6 in Dubai right now. I don't know for sure if I want to become a professional chef, but I do know that baking will always, always be my hobby.

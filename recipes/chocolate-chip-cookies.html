Oven Magic

Chocolate Chip Cookies

The recipe that turns a normal afternoon into a party — the bake that never felt like practice, and the one Vedhica shared at her Mamalu Kitchen birthday.

Prep
15 min
Bake
~12 min
Makes
~12
Level
Easy

Ingredients

Tap each item as you go.

  • 1¼ cups all-purpose flour
  • ½ teaspoon baking soda
  • A pinch of salt
  • ½ cup melted butter
  • ½ cup brown sugar
  • ¼ cup white sugar
  • 1 egg
  • ½ teaspoon vanilla extract
  • ¾ cup chocolate chips

Method

  1. Prep

    Preheat the oven and line a baking tray with parchment paper.

  2. Dry mix

    Whisk the flour, baking soda, and salt together in one bowl.

  3. Wet mix

    In another bowl, mix the melted butter, brown sugar, and white sugar until smooth, then beat in the egg and vanilla.

  4. Combine

    Pour the wet mixture into the dry ingredients and stir just until combined — a few small lumps are fine.

  5. Add the chips

    Fold in the chocolate chips.

  6. Scoop

    Scoop rounded spoonfuls onto the tray, spaced apart since they'll spread. This is the step Vedhica still reminds herself about every time.

  7. Bake

    Bake until the edges are golden but the centres still look a little soft, then let them cool on the tray before touching them.

Vedhica's note: not every bake has to teach you something new. Sometimes the value is in doing something familiar well, over and over, until it just becomes something you know how to make.

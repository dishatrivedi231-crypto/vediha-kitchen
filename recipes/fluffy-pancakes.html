Fluffy Buttermilk Pancakes

Tall, tender and golden with lightly crisp edges — this is the cosy stack that makes any morning feel like a treat. No fancy tricks, just one big bowl.

Prep
10 min
Cook
10 min
Makes
8 pancakes
Level
Easy

Ingredients

Tap each item as you go.

  • 1½ cups (190g) plain flour
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp baking soda
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • 1¼ cups (300ml) buttermilk
  • 1 large egg
  • 3 tbsp melted butter
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Butter or oil, for the pan

Method

  1. Mix the dry

    In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda and salt.

  2. Mix the wet

    In a jug, whisk the buttermilk, egg, melted butter and vanilla until smooth.

  3. Bring together — gently

    Pour the wet into the dry and stir just until combined. A few lumps are perfect — overmixing makes flat pancakes. Rest the batter 5 minutes.

  4. Cook

    Heat a lightly buttered pan on medium. Pour ¼ cup batter per pancake. Cook until bubbles form on top and the edges look set (~2 min), then flip and cook 1–2 minutes more.

  5. Stack & serve

    Keep warm in a low oven while you cook the rest. Serve tall with butter, maple syrup and berries.

Vedhica's tip: No buttermilk? Stir 1 tbsp lemon juice into 1¼ cups milk and wait 5 minutes — instant homemade buttermilk.

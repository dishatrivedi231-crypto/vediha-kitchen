Oven Magic

Classic Vanilla Cupcakes

Soft, buttery little sponges crowned with silky vanilla buttercream. This is the one to master first — sweet, simple and always a hit.

Prep
15 min
Bake
20 min
Makes
12 cupcakes
Level
Easy

Ingredients

Tap each item as you go.

For the cupcakes

  • 1½ cups (190g) plain flour
  • 1½ tsp baking powder
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • ½ cup (115g) butter, softened
  • 1 cup (200g) sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • ½ cup (120ml) milk

For the buttercream

  • ¾ cup (170g) butter, softened
  • 2½ cups (300g) icing sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1–2 tbsp milk

Method

  1. Prep

    Heat the oven to 180°C (350°F). Line a 12-hole muffin tin with paper cases.

  2. Cream butter & sugar

    Beat the softened butter and sugar for 2–3 minutes until pale and fluffy. This is what makes them light.

  3. Add eggs & vanilla

    Beat in the eggs one at a time, then the vanilla, until smooth.

  4. Fold in flour & milk

    Whisk the flour, baking powder and salt together, then add to the batter in two goes, alternating with the milk. Mix just until combined.

  5. Bake

    Divide between the cases (about ⅔ full). Bake 18–20 minutes, until a skewer comes out clean and the tops spring back. Cool completely.

  6. Make the buttercream

    Beat the butter until creamy, add the icing sugar gradually, then the vanilla and enough milk to reach a soft, pipeable swirl. Beat 2 minutes until fluffy.

  7. Swirl & decorate

    Pipe or spoon onto the cooled cupcakes and finish with sprinkles. Done!

Vedhica's tip: Ingredients at room temperature blend far better — take the butter, eggs and milk out about 30 minutes before you start.

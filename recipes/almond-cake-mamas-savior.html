Almond Cake — "Mama's Savior"

The no-sugar, no-egg cake that saves the celebration. Built from almonds for richness and dates for sweetness — nothing that would leave Vedhica's mum out of her own party.

Prep
15 min
Bake
~30 min
Serves
8
Level
Medium

Ingredients

Tap each item as you go.

  • 1½ cups almond flour — holds the cake together instead of regular flour
  • 6–8 soft dates, pitted and blended into a paste — the sweetener, no sugar needed
  • 3 tablespoons yogurt or mashed banana — does the job eggs usually do
  • ¼ cup melted butter or oil
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder — gives it a bit of rise since there's no egg
  • ½ teaspoon vanilla extract
  • A pinch of salt — balances the sweetness
  • A handful of chopped almonds, for the top

Method

  1. Prep

    Preheat the oven and grease a small cake tin.

  2. Make the date paste

    Blend the dates with a splash of warm water until it turns into a smooth paste.

  3. Mix the wet

    Mix the date paste with the yogurt (or mashed banana), melted butter, and vanilla.

  4. Fold in the dry

    Fold in the almond flour, baking powder, and salt, and stir just until it comes together.

  5. Bake

    Pour the batter into the tin, sprinkle the chopped almonds on top, and bake until a toothpick comes out clean.

  6. Cool fully

    Let it cool all the way before slicing. It holds together so much better once it's rested.

Vedhica's note: the cakes people remember most aren't always the fanciest ones. Sometimes the one that matters most is just the one that makes sure everyone at the table gets a slice.

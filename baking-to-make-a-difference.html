How it began

Vedhica loved baking every day — but the cakes usually just got eaten at home or shared with friends at birthday parties. Since they were really tasty, she had a simple thought: why not donate to some charities? They might want some delicious treats too.

Her first charity bake was 50 cupcakes for the Superheroes and Blue Umbrella event on July 3. It's the newest chapter of her story — and the one that keeps growing every time she bakes.

A little recognition

The Blue Umbrella gave Vedhica a certificate for volunteering — "celebrating a young heart whose thoughtful cupcakes made our special children feel loved and cherished."

Where it goes next

This page will keep a running tally as new events are added, so you can watch the number climb. Have an event or a cause that could use a batch of cupcakes? A grown-up looks after all the arrangements — reach out below and it'll land in a parent's inbox.

A note for grown-ups: Vedhica is a young baker, so a parent manages all contact, arrangements and any charity proceeds through an adult-controlled account. Messages from this site go to a parent's inbox, not directly to her.
